0. Why Write?

As a researcher and analyst, a creator of content ultimately, this is a strange time. While the cost of making new content has become lower than ever, we've seen a deluge of billions of ways to spend our attenion. Now that AI can produce images, videos, music, writing, analytics, and the very code that is software and even AI, well, we humans might start to feel like the waters are rising. In such a time, we must not stand still, but zoom out and critically think about this noisy landscape of content. While I may contribute some content-water here, as the Analytics on this website reflect the power of AI-assisted Development, I will hand-write blog entries the old-fashioned way, on a Bluetooth Ergonomic Keyboard.

I posit this truism: that the powers of LLMs are unlocked by the prompts of its intentioned users. For now, these users are humans with missions and goals and drives to produce value for humans. Yet in our quest for value-production, amidst all this amazing content, we are unable to take true rest for ourselves, and thus naturally less attention is now paid to writing skills than it was in recent decades.

Why write? Firstly, it's healthy for thought: writing develops the mind's capacity to thinking, it is a meta-technology that helps the writer think better about the world. Secondly, those thoughts when well-written may become valuable to others, contributing to our community of human thought. Writing well helps us connect the dots to seek areas of consensus and coalescence with fellow minds. And elephant in the room: writing well helps interface with AI and LLMs, thus improving our steering ability on the fast-moving AI ship.

Thanks so much for reading. I look forward to writing my thoughts here, guaranteed 100% Homo Sapiens, giving you purely human takes via purely human-written essays.
