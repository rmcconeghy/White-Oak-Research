3. AI for Data Analytics

November 18, 2026

GPT 6.2 is fresh out of the data center, and here are Data Analysis Benchmarks by Plotly https://plotly.com/blog/gpt-6-2-sol-plotly-data-analytics-bench/

I applaud the great analytics done on AI models in this blog post. We are now using AI to do Analytics about using AI for Analytics. This is a great read and I believe essential reading for all data folks today.

So, the famous Python library plotly maintains this 43 question secret analytics exam and GPT 6.2, SOTA as of 11/17/26, just passed the exam with 93% or 40/43 for $1.22. It's the fastest A-grade model and the 3rd cheapest for their exam. I'd be curious to take the test myself, but it is not public because naturally, the AIs could train on the benchmark easily. Side note, one instance of AI models tuning to common benchmarks might be Simon Willison's pelican on a bike drawing over on the HN forums.

Overall, the take-away from Plotly is that these models seem to be getting faster (10.5 seconds per question), and cheaper, with a 43 question exam costing only $1.22. The models were doing these fast analytics on a 19.9 M row data warehouse spanning 44 tables and 554 columns, 11 seconds per question: It's quite a performance!
