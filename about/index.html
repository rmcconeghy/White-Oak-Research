ABOUT

White Oak Research

I started White Oak Research to show what one researcher can do with public healthcare data and AI-assisted analytics in 2026.

Get in touch

I started coding in R in the 2000s and went on to use Python, SAS, Stata, Ruby, and SQL in healthcare research. I spent years learning the statistics and epidemiology I needed to design studies and analyze the results. Even with that experience, I’m surprised by how quickly I can explore a healthcare question with AI-assisted workflows today.

I can spend less time looking up syntax each time I switch languages or frameworks. That leaves more time for the parts of research that depend on knowing the subject well. When I map primary care, for example, I have to decide who counts as a primary care provider and which population to use when comparing provider density across regions.

When an analysis takes fewer hours, it costs less to try the next one. I can start with a map of dialysis centers, then use public transit schedules to ask which centers a patient could reach for an early appointment. That’s what excites me about combining public data with AI-assisted development. More of the questions we have about healthcare can become studies we can actually do and share.
