About White Oak Research

I’m Rob McConeghy, a healthcare researcher and analyst based in Rhode Island. White Oak Research is my independent research and writing project. I explore healthcare access, spending, and other questions using public data, drawing on my background in healthcare research, statistics, and analytics.

I do the research and use AI to help write code, test ideas, and build the figures you see here. I’m responsible for the analytical decisions and for reviewing the results.

I started coding in R in the 2000s and went on to use Python, SAS, Stata, Ruby, and SQL in healthcare research. AI speeds up the programming. The decisions I spent years learning in statistics and epidemiology still shape every study.

Take the dialysis map. One bus can stop three times near the same clinic. Counting those stops as three trips would make service look more frequent than it is. I publish my methods so readers can see where decisions like that enter the analysis.
