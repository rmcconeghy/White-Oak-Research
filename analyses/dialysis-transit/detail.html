Methods and limitations

Include all 16 CMS Rhode Island facilities offering in-center hemodialysis. Use CMS 22 Baker Street for CCN 413500 as directed; the provider address discrepancy remains documented. Join the supplied offline coordinates to CMS using CCN as text; require one-to-one coverage of all 16 clinics, normalized street/ZIP agreement, finite coordinates and inclusion in the Rhode Island boundary. Coordinates are Census street-address geocodes accepted as the analysis points, not independently verified buildings or patient entrances. The offline CSV does not provide its retrieval date; none is inferred. Preserve CMS unit information even where geocoding omits the unit. No ZIP centroids or inferred zeros.

Activate service for November 17 and 21 using GTFS calendars and exceptions. This snapshot is exceptions-only: calendar_dates.txt supplies every active service date. Tuesday has 2,374 active trips; Saturday 1,515. Times use America/New_York. Tuesday is a regular weekday example, not a weekday average; Thursday has additional service.

Calculate spherical straight-line distances using a 6,371,008.8 m Earth radius. Apply active-date, stopping-location, distance ≤400 m, permitted drop-off, and arrival ≥05:00:00 and <08:00:00 filters before COUNT(DISTINCT trip_id). Repeat at 800 m. Extended GTFS times stay extended. No frequency file occurs in this snapshot.

Retain every tie at the highest and lowest count among validated clinics. Flag a substantial ranking change whenever a clinic enters or leaves a highest/lowest group in the Saturday or 800 m scenarios. Do not impose an arbitrary numerical tie-break. With missing locations, extremes are partial and statewide conclusions are withheld.

The map boundary is an administrative footprint including coastal waters, not a land-only shoreline. Map numbers identify clinics; teal marks all highest ties, rust all lowest ties and gray the other counts.

Supporting hourly departures use pickup eligibility and a separate filter for each hour before deduplication. A trip may occur in multiple hours. First/last departure spans include all eligible nearby stop events and preserve 24+ times. These departure measures do not define the arrival ranking.

Transit supply near a clinic does not establish travel feasibility from patient origins, transfers, safe walking routes, treatment-time compatibility, reliability or individual mobility needs. Saturday bus service does not imply Saturday treatment is offered.
