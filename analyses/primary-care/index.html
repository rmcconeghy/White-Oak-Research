Methods and limitations

The numerator counts distinct, active individual physicians in NPPES whose primary taxonomy is family medicine, general practice, internal medicine, or pediatrics. The analysis used their listed primary practice locations and geographically assigned 92.77% of filtered providers.

Rates use 2024 American Community Survey population estimates and 2025 Census TIGER/Line county boundaries, including Connecticut’s nine planning regions. The map’s color scale ends at 20.8 so the Suffolk outlier does not obscure differences elsewhere. The rankings retain its actual value.

These rates describe where physicians practice. They cannot establish how many accept new patients, how long a patient waits, or where patients travel for care.
